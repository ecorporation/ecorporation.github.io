About Orbitrary

Orbitrary is an independent research site focused on satellite security, spacecraft systems, orbital mechanics, and Hack-A-Sat.

Purpose

The goal is to go beyond ordinary challenge write-ups. Each article examines the background theory, experiments, failed approaches, security assumptions, and final solution.

Topics

  • Hack-A-Sat challenge research
  • Orbital mechanics
  • Attitude determination and control
  • Telemetry and command protocols
  • Flight software
  • Radio and ground systems
  • Reverse engineering

Website Design

This website intentionally uses the visual language of early graphical web portals. The interface is obsolete by design.
